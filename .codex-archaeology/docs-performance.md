# TinyDraw documents / receipts archaeology and performance ledger

Research date: 2026-08-19. This report is documentary archaeology, not a new benchmark run. No firmware was flashed and no historical tree was rebuilt. Unless a row says otherwise, the Vector V2 device is the Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED 1.8 V2: ESP32-S3 240 MHz, 8 MiB octal PSRAM (80 MHz), CO5300 368x448 RGB565 panel at **40 MHz effective**. The 40 MHz fact matters: every historical configuration called “50 MHz” or “60 MHz” was also 40 MHz actual because of the GPSPI divider (`docs/receipts/hardware/CO5300_PANEL_LIMITS_2026-08-15.md:30-51`).

## Evidence rules used here

- **A2A Y**: same corpus, metric endpoint, hardware, revision/layout, and configuration, preferably an alternating or one-image A/B.
- **A2A P**: operation and corpus align, but build/layout, run distribution, or endpoint differs in a material way.
- **A2A N**: different operation, corpus, platform, endpoint, or a physical premise later falsified.
- Evidence confidence: **C** = CONFIRMED by a direct receipt/raw artifact or same-image gate; **H** = HIGH CONFIDENCE INFERENCE from consistent summaries; **U** = UNCERTAIN.
- Publication classification follows the requested vocabulary: **VERIFIED**, **PROBABLY VALID**, **MISLEADING WITHOUT CONTEXT**, **DO NOT USE**. “Verified” applies only to the explicitly described metric, not a broader product claim.
- The current tree intentionally removed 377 raw logs/transcripts/binaries/videos/frames (68,217,490 bytes). They remain byte-for-byte at tag `v2-feature-complete-pre-cleanup`; receipt paths to removed artifacts refer to that tag (`docs/EVIDENCE_ARCHIVE.md:3-17`). New receipts are expected to retain SHA/build/setup and the smallest counterexample (`docs/ARTIFACT_RETENTION.md:1-22`).

## Hardware and memory facts that changed the mental model

1. **The panel was slower than the software believed.** Requested 50/60 MHz was always 40 MHz actual; writer and modeled beam were near parity (27.2 vs 26.7 rows/ms), not 1.53x separated. Full-frame payload alone is 16.487 ms, longer than the 16.773 ms TE period; edge-synced full-frame cadence therefore tops out at 29.4 FPS. This invalidates the numerical premise behind beam racing (`CO5300_PANEL_LIMITS...md:30-101,114-134`).
2. **The scanline register was not an oracle.** GETSCANLINE and every control-read probe returned zero; only optical capture could adjudicate tearing (`CO5300_PANEL_LIMITS...md:103-112`). The later Block B positive control tore while rising-edge full-frame cadence was clean across 1,495 analyzed frames (`...md:146-152`).
3. **Flash layout is a performance variable.** Unrelated code changed an internal byte-swap loop 11 -> 17–18 us and uniformly raised strip staging 2–4%. IRAM-pinning panel transport changed strip-8 mean/max 660/~700 -> 633/647 us and jitter 40 -> 13 us. The documented build-to-build cold law is ±2–3% (`cold-stage-b.../RECEIPT.md:82-100,180-190`).
4. **PSRAM placement is also a variable, but not universally.** A 40 KiB settled workspace mid-heap cost ~9 ms cold; dead-last placement removed it (`docs/MEMORY_MAP.md:19-23`). Conversely, the controlled 2026-08-12 scratch A/B showed moving the main settled coverage scratch internal improved total wall only 1.69%, falsifying the “PSRAM is the renderer bottleneck” theory (`PROTOTYPE_EXIT.md:31-57`).
5. **Final memory is intentionally packed.** PSRAM: four full surfaces 1,318,912 B; 604 tiles 4,947,968 B; authority 720,000 B; metadata/scratch/index/workspace fill most of 8 MiB; ~870,792 B free. Internal steady product uses ~100,039 B IRAM text and funds 32 KiB producer surface, 12,384 B chord plans, two 23,552 B DMA buffers; export dips to ~84–90 KiB free. Flash is fully partitioned: 1,792 KiB app, 4 MiB journal, 10.125 MiB FAT export, 64 KiB coredump (`docs/MEMORY_MAP.md:1-55`).
6. **Cache versus history is a deliberate compromise.** Preserved Undo preimages are evicted before current LRU tiles; pan/zoom retention always beats Undo runway. Heavy navigation converts fast history swaps into exact rebuilds, but history can never evict current pan tiles (`docs/MEMORY_MAP.md:57-65`).
